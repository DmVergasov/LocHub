# Support and License

## Support

Questions, bug reports and feature requests: **rim2812@gmail.com**.

When reporting a problem, please include:

- The Unreal Engine version (5.6, 5.7 or 5.8) and platform (Windows, macOS or Linux) you are on.
- What you were doing (Push, Pull, a translation job, a Grid/Review/Glossary action, the commandlet, …).
- The exact error message, if any.
- If it involves the LocHub service, the relevant lines from `Saved/LocHub/service.log`.
